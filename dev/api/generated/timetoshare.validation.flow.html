timetoshare.validation.flow#

Flow rules: flow control, FlowMaps and virtual timelines.

The schema checks the shape of every flow-control element, FlowMap and virtual timeline; the rules here reach beyond it.

Before the schema, an element written in the earlier form of flow control (the key type with jump_from, jump_to or flow_marker, or the key target) is refused with the name of the converter (flow_control_form).

After the schema, timeline by timeline, the rules read the elements that are not rejected. An element is rejected when an entry of its provenance holds a belief false: a repair rejects an element and adds another, so a rejected jump and its replacement may share a coordinate. In document order: a coordinate lies from 0 to the timeline’s known length (flow_coordinate); a timeline has one jump at a coordinate (jump_repeated); the destinations of a jump are distinct places (jump_destination); a to names a jump target or a region of the timeline, a child of it or the timeline itself (dangling_target); a place reached by by lies from 0 to the known length (flow_coordinate); an after names exactly one destination of another jump of the timeline (dangling_target).

Then the FlowMaps: timeline names a root (flow_map_timeline), every chunk ID a region or a child timeline inside that root’s tree, or a virtual timeline of a timeline of it (flow_map_chunk), and at most one FlowMap of a timeline is canonical (flow_map_canonical); and the virtual timelines: map names a FlowMap or a conversion map, timeline a timeline or another virtual timeline (dangling_target). A virtual timeline comes, virtual timeline by virtual timeline, from a timeline: none comes from itself through others. Where its map is a FlowMap and it states no through, the timeline it comes from lies in the tree of the FlowMap’s root, and its length is the sum of the FlowMap’s chunks and gaps where all of them lie on its own axis (virtual_timeline); with through neither is read.

A manifestation or a convention outside the format’s vocabulary is valid: flow_warnings() reports it.

Yield every flow-control element written in the earlier form.

Root by root, each timeline’s elements before its children’s. The walk reads any value and either form of an ID.

timetoshare.validation.flow.flow_map_problems(manifest)[source]#

Yield the problems of the FlowMaps, then of the virtual timelines.

FlowMap by FlowMap: its timeline, its chunks in their order (none where the timeline is no root), then a second canonical FlowMap of its timeline. Virtual timeline by virtual timeline: its map, then its timeline, then the virtual timelines it comes from itself through, and, where it comes from a timeline and applies a FlowMap of a root without through, the FlowMap of another root, else its length.

timetoshare.validation.flow.flow_warnings(manifest)[source]#

Return the flow warnings of a valid manifest, in document order.

manifestation_unknown at the manifestation of a flow-control element or of a jump’s destination that the format does not define for it, and convention_unknown at the convention of a FlowMap that is none of the format’s.
